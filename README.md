# 📊 Experience in Analytical Reporting, Data Integration, and Storytelling

This repository presents a selection of my work in **Business Intelligence**, **data integration**, and **analytical storytelling**, developed in both the **public and private sectors**.

I use **Power BI**, **SQL (MySQL, Oracle, PostgreSQL)**, **Python**, and **Dremio Data Lakehouse** to turn complex data into clear, consistent, decision-oriented visual narratives.

---

## 🏛️ Government Projects – Education and Health

I developed many of these reports while working as a **Data Analyst at Brazil’s Ministries of Education and Health**, supporting data intelligence and governance initiatives focused on **health workforce training**, **medical residency programs**, and **federal program management**.

### Key Activities

- Built **executive dashboards** in **Power BI**, using advanced **DAX**, **RLS/OLS**, semantic modeling, and analytical storytelling for senior decision-makers.
- Wrote complex **SQL queries** in **MySQL** and **PostgreSQL**.
- Integrated multiple sources in **Dremio Data Lakehouse**, with data curation and governance.
- Automated data pipelines with **Python (Pandas and web scraping)** to collect and process public data.
- Created **views**, **ETL processes**, and analytical layers to consolidate data from multiple sources.

### Examples of Deliverables

- Dashboards monitoring **medical and multidisciplinary residency programs**.
- Reports on participation and oversight of federal programs, including **PET-Saúde, Mais Médicos, CAMEM, and Prodeps**.
- Dashboards tracking the expansion and decentralization of health workforce training.

> Some reports are for internal use and have restricted access. The versions published on GitHub use publicly available data and are designed to demonstrate technical skills and visual storytelling.

## 💼 Private-Sector Projects – InnLeaders Consulting

At **InnLeaders Consulting**, I worked as a **Data Analytics Consultant**, developing analytical solutions and corporate reports for industrial and business clients.

My work brought together rigorous analysis, effective data design, and analytical storytelling to support evidence-based business decisions.

### Key Deliverables

- Developed **financial and operational performance dashboards** in **Power BI**, with a focus on executive storytelling.
- Wrote complex **SQL queries** in **Oracle**.
- Created narrative dashboards presenting production, efficiency, and industrial sustainability KPIs.

These experiences strengthened my ability to translate analytical results into clear, visual, and useful narratives across different organizational contexts.
